We work in three ways — separately or together. In each, we design and build working prototypes and pilots rather than recommendations, so teams develop real capability and belief, not just a plan.

Align

Executive AI Blueprint

A focused sprint to align leadership on AI vision, governance, and highest-ROI use cases. We make the vision tangible — not just a roadmap, but a working prototype that shows what's possible.

Enable

AI Champion Program

Cohort-based enablement that trains internal leaders to build and run AI tools from the inside. Modeled on the systems behind Airbnb's Superhost program.

Embed

Culture & Behavior Embedding

Ongoing advisory to redesign incentives, rituals, and KPIs so adoption becomes self-sustaining. The goal isn't a successful pilot — it's an organization that keeps moving.
